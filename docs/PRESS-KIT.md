# Press kit

Everything a reviewer, blogger, or curator needs to write about Decaffeinate —
no need to ask.

## The one-liner

**Decaffeinate is the Mac utility built to make your Mac sleep — not just stay
awake.** It shows you exactly what's keeping your Mac up, and gives you the
power to put it to sleep anyway — even when a rogue app, a stray `caffeinate`
process, or a background tab won't let go.

## Boilerplate (use freely)

> Decaffeinate is a free, open-source macOS menu-bar app that tells you the
> truth about what's keeping your Mac awake — attributed to the real
> process, with a plain-English reason — and force-sleeps an idle Mac even
> when something is quietly holding it up. It's built for the agentic-coding
> era: AI agents like Claude Code and Cursor (rightly) hold the Mac awake
> mid-task, but nothing used to tell you when that hold outlived its purpose.
> Decaffeinate runs entirely in user space (no root, no kernel extension, no
> telemetry) and is MIT-licensed.

## Facts

- **Developer:** [Harf Promo](https://github.com/harf-promo)
- **Platform:** macOS 14+ (Intel + Apple Silicon, universal binary)
- **Price:** Free
- **License:** MIT, open source
- **Distribution:** [Homebrew cask](https://github.com/harf-promo/homebrew-tap), signed + notarized DMG
- **Repository:** <https://github.com/harf-promo/decaffeinate>
- **Trust boundary:** no root, no private APIs, no telemetry, no accounts — see [`ARCHITECTURE.md`](ARCHITECTURE.md)
